Why I offer this

Life is a precious gift, and it is meant to be lived joyfully. Yet there are times when something stands in the way: a personal struggle, the loss of a job or of a loved one, a restless mind, or a situation you cannot quite name or understand. In those times, enjoying life can feel very far away.

Our mental health is at the heart of our wellbeing. It shapes how we think, feel, work and love, and it deserves the same care as our physical health. Hard times weigh on the mind and the heart, and no one should have to carry them alone.

I started Iksoi to walk with people through those times, so that they can find their way back to a happy and joyful life. There is a better side of life than running from one demand to the next, and I want to make it easier for you to see. I do this because I genuinely want to help people and to do the best I can for a better world. I cannot change everything, but I can offer my time, my attention and a kind ear, one conversation at a time.

I am not a therapist, and when you need more than a conversation, I will gladly point you to the right professional. I am a real person, not an AI. For now the conversations are free and there is no obligation.

What a session is

A session is a private conversation, one person at a time. It usually lasts 45 minutes and takes place by video or voice call. You choose what we talk about, whether it is something personal, the pressure of studies and exams, or anything else that stands in the way of a peaceful life.

It is

  • A place to say what is on your mind without being judged, however heavy it feels.
  • Someone who listens first, then asks questions that help you see things differently.
  • Small, practical ideas you can try the same day, such as a breathing exercise or a question to sit with.

It is not

  • Therapy, counselling or medical treatment.
  • Professional financial or legal advice.
  • A diagnosis, or a cure for any condition.
  • A replacement for a doctor, therapist or other professional.
  • Help in an emergency.

How to begin

  1. Write to me

    Send a short email about what is on your mind. A few lines are enough.

  2. We find a time

    I reply myself, usually within three days, and we agree a time that suits you.

  3. We talk

    We meet online for one conversation. If it helps, we can meet again.

When you need more than a conversation

Some things call for a trained professional. If you are thinking about harming yourself, if you feel unsafe, or if low mood, anxiety, pressure from studies or worries about money have taken over your days, please reach out to a qualified professional or a helpline in your own country. I am not a licensed professional or a certified practitioner, and I cannot replace one.

In immediate danger
Call your local emergency number: 112 in India, Europe and many other countries, 911 in the United States and Canada, 999 in the United Kingdom, 000 in Australia.
In India
112 reaches police, fire and ambulance across India. To talk to someone, call Tele-MANAS on 14416 or 1800-891-4416. It is the government's free mental health helpline, open 24 hours in many languages (telemanas.mohfw.gov.in). Childline is free, open 24 hours, and is for children and for adults calling on a child's behalf: 1098.
Someone to talk to, in your country
findahelpline.com lists free, confidential helplines in more than 130 countries, by country and by topic. Befrienders Worldwide is a network of crisis helplines in 35 countries.
A licensed professional near you
Ask your doctor, hospital or health insurer for a referral. Choose a psychologist, psychiatrist, therapist or counsellor who is licensed or registered where you live, and check this with your national or regional professional body. Psychology Today lists verified therapists in more than 20 countries.
Money worries
Free debt and money advice exists in many countries. Search for free debt advice together with the name of your country, or ask your town hall, a local welfare organisation or a citizens' advice service.

These services are run by independent organisations, and Iksoi is not connected with them. I read my email, but not around the clock, so please do not use it in an emergency.
